Insomnia and the Daydreamer

5 documented callbacks Directed by Tiffany Johnson Written by Des Moran & Ashley Laverne Jackson 39 years after the original premiere

Deborah starts questioning whether the man she's been imagining as her ideal partner is actually right for her. Her unresolved feelings and romantic options become increasingly difficult to ignore. Meanwhile, the students go out for a night at a club, only for the evening to become chaotic when the aunties unexpectedly arrive. The resulting night mixes the younger characters' romantic drama with the older generation's unfinished business.

S5·E106 — “Twelve Steps of Christmas” · first aired 1991-12-19
By the original's fifth season its core women were married and negotiating holidays as families — the Taylors blending traditions, Dwayne and Whitley circling each other.

'The aunties' is the joke with a knife in it: the original's students are now the women who show up at the club. The 2026 show uses them as a generation the kids cannot dismiss — because they were the ones on the dancefloor first.

S1·E1 — “Reconcilable Differences” · first aired 1987-09-24
Jaleesa Vinson is Denise's first roommate: a 26-year-old divorcée finishing her degree, treated as a scandal by rumour, and eventually the person who captains Gilbert Hall's step team and marries Col. Taylor.

Jaleesa's whole story was about being older, poorer and more serious than the rest of the room. The revival's students are financially squeezed; the auntie with the least patience for their excuses is the one who did it while broke.

S6·E120 — “Honeymoon in L.A. (part 1)” · first aired 1992-09-24
Jenifer Lewis first appears in the original's sixth-season opener as Dean Davenport, listed in the episode's production notes among the new recurring cast. She is the institution's authority figure: faculty above the students and not paid to be liked.

What the episode is about

The older generation shows up uninvited and refuses to be a cameo: the episode is about what the alumnae carry and what they demand.

The original said it first — S5·E95 “We've Only Just Begun” (1991-09-19)

Jaleesa and Col. Taylor reveal they eloped over the summer; Dwayne asks Ron to collect Whitley's engagement ring and he brings back the wrong one. The original's relationships kept resolving off-screen and inconveniently.

What is happening now
  • Intergenerational wealth transfer is the mechanism that makes racial wealth gaps persist or close; mentorship and family networks are the non-financial half of that transfer. Source: context “racial-inequality”, 2026-10-04
  • Black women in the US reach midlife with a fraction of the retirement wealth of White peers — the 'aunties' at the club are also the generation carrying the family's finances. Source: context “racial-inequality”, 2026-10-04
Our reading — interpretation, not fact

Comedies love to bring the old cast back for a laugh. This show's version of that is sharper: the women who were the students are now the standard the students are judged against, and they have unfinished business of their own. The political content is inheritance — what one generation of Black women owes, and refuses to hand over to, the next.
