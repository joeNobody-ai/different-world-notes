A Different World is an American comedy television series and a revival of the 1987–1993 NBC sitcom of the same title. The series stars Maleah Joi Moon, with Jasmine Guy and Kadeem Hardison reprising their roles from the original series billed as "special guest stars" alongside many other returning actors. It premiered on Netflix on September 24, 2026, exactly 39 years from the original series premiere date. It received generally positive reviews from critics. In October 2026, the series was renewed for a second season.


== Premise ==
When Dwayne Wayne and Whitley Gilbert's free-spirited and kindhearted, yet rebellious youngest child Deborah enters her freshman year at Hillman College, she finds her parents' shadow difficult to escape as she aims to build her own legacy. She does that while also having the time of her life with the new generation of Hillman's finest and brightest.

== Production ==


=== Development ===
On August 23 and 24, 2012, Debbie Allen wrote on Twitter that she wanted to reboot the series. Over a million people on Facebook, Twitter, and blogs reacted to the tweet and approved of the potential reboot.
On August 7, 2024, Deadline reported that a sequel to the series was in the works at Netflix: a single-camera half-hour comedy focusing on original cast characters Whitley & Dwayne's daughter, who also attends Hillman College. Deadline indicated that the series would be written and produced by Felicia Pride, who has worked on Bel-Air and Grey's Anatomy, and that Debbie Allen would be an executive producer, along with Mandy Summers, Tom Werner, Gina Prince-Bythewood and Reggie Bythewood. On March 28, 2025, Netflix ordered a pilot of the sequel series. On October 2, 2026, Netflix renewed the show for a second season.


=== Filming ===
Production was delayed by the COVID-19 pandemic. Principal photography began in Atlanta in February 2026 and wrapped in May 2026.

== Reception ==
The review aggregator website Rotten Tomatoes reported a 82% approval rating based on 22 critic reviews. The website's critics consensus reads, "This revived continuation of a beloved classic follows new characters through the lens of Gen-Z, crackling with energy, strutting with style, and written with full-hearted verve, proving that A Different World isn't such a bad thing after all." Metacritic, which uses a weighted average, assigned a score of 72 out of 100, based on 13 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.
