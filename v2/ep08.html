Our reading — interpretation, not fact

Not every episode carries a political thesis, and pretending otherwise is how fan writing becomes propaganda for a show the writers never made. The storm is a dramaturgical device the original established in 1991. Its politics are downstream of whatever the trapped characters finally say to each other.
